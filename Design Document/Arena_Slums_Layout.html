Slum Arena Layout

Redesign: the court is gone, and the arena is all alleys now, 4.5 m wide, on a 1.5 m grid so the 3 m wall kit fits. 54 × 48 m. Every alley bends or ends in a T within 10–20 m, so the longest line of sight is about 25 m (a 1.2 m-wide line, measured across the whole map) instead of 40+ m with the court. Eight dead-end alleys still end in shanty doors where enemies come out. The +4 m gallery is now a balcony on the south face of one house. Both its stairs come down facing north along an open alley, with 5.5 m and 11.5 m of lane ahead. No grenade launcher.

You come down the stairs facing north into the widened west alley, not into a wall. The east stair is the mirror of this one, with 11.5 m of alley ahead running up to the north lane. The deck is the house's second floor (+4 m) and covers the north 3 m of the lane below, with 3.6 m of headroom under it. The rifle is up there, and so is a view down three alleys. Both stairs lead up to it, so it's a loop, not a dead end.

How it plays

  • Kill 20 to clear it. 4 are out at once and each kill brings another, so the last 4 come out with nobody behind them.
  • You start in the east-middle alley with no long views, so the first contact is close.
  • Enemies come out of doors you can't see and arrive round corners at 5–15 m. That's pistol and shotgun range, with dives across T-junctions.
  • The gallery is the one high spot. Take the rifle, hold for a moment, and get off before they reach both stairs.
  • Fewer open spaces means fewer enemies converging on you at once. Any group has to come down a 4.5 m alley.

Layout calls I made

  • The longest 25 m lines are shallow diagonals through a bend. Straight runs end at 18 m or less.
  • The jeepney (south lane) and the kiosk (west junction) each leave a 2.1 m squeeze to walk past, and each cuts one of the longer views.
  • The 1.5 m-thick pieces (B17, B19, B21, B22) are 4 m boundary walls rather than houses.
  • The block table is a plain split of the solid area. Neighbouring pieces touch, so walls go only on faces that border an alley.
