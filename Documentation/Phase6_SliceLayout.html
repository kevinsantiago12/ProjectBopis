Slice Blockout Plan

The six-beat run from Phase6_SliceBrief.md, laid out to scale. Linear, room encounters, about 5–8 minutes. Every doorway is drawn 3 m wide (brief minimum 2 m) so a dive through it never clips. Place, faction and story are left abstract on purpose.

Build checks

  • Doorways ≥ 2 m (drawn 3 m); ceilings ≥ 3.5 m everywhere for the dive camera, including under the balcony (3.6 m).
  • One NavMesh bounds volume over the whole run, balcony and stairwell included.
  • Room 4's wave door and back corridor stay out of the player's view until the wave arrives.
  • Only new code: the exit trigger. Checkpoints only if a full restart proves too punishing.

Layout calls I made

  • Room 0 ends straight in room 1's wide doorway. The brief said "closed or narrow door", which fights the dive-in entry.
  • Room 3's grenades sit in room 2's side room, so taking them is a small detour.
  • Room 5 is entered from the balcony, so the climb in room 4 is forced and the launcher is on the way.
  • Cover blocks are low and few, placed to dive between, never to peek from.
